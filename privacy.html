Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Bicoria is published by Really Useful Apps. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: it doesn't collect any.

What stays on your device

Your bicycles, photographs, odometer readings, service journal, parts, schedules and settings are stored only on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, in a local database. The app has no server, no accounts and no sign-in. I never receive your data.

If you use a Workshop, it can also hold customer references, notes about jobs and the names of your team members. They are stored on the device in the same way and are never sent anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • No sharing or selling of data, because none is collected.
  • No location access and no network connections of its own.

Permissions

  • Camera and photo library: only when you choose to add a photograph of a bicycle. Photographs are copied into the app and stay on your device.
  • Notifications: to remind you when maintenance is due. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Backups

The app keeps a backup copy on your device. You can also save a copy wherever you choose, for example your own iCloud Drive, using your device's standard share and file features. That copy belongs to you and is held by the provider you chose under its own privacy policy. I cannot see or access it. Your device's own backup features (such as iCloud device backup) may also include app data under Apple's terms.

Diagnostic log

The app keeps a small log on your device to help fix faults. It records IDs, counts and error codes, not your bicycles' names, your notes or your team's names. It leaves your device only if you press Share and send it yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Settings, then Delete all my data, removes everything Bicoria holds on your device, including its backup copies and photographs. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Delete any copy you saved elsewhere yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
