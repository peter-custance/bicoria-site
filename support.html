Support

Questions, problems or ideas for Bicoria? Email reallyusefulapps@outlook.com.

Useful to know

  • About (Bike Shed, then the three-dot menu) explains what the app is for and how it looks after your data.
  • If something goes wrong, Bike Shed, three-dot menu, Diagnostics lets you share the app's log with me. The log holds IDs, counts and error codes, never your bicycles' names, notes or team names.
  • Your data lives only on your device. Bicoria keeps a backup copy whenever you leave the app; in Settings you can save a copy somewhere else, such as iCloud Drive, and restore it on a new device. I cannot recover data for you.
  • Distances can be shown in miles or kilometres (Settings). Switching never changes what you recorded.

Join the test

Thank you for helping to test Bicoria before it is released. You need an iPhone or iPad running iOS 15 or later, or a Mac running macOS 14 or later.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store (Apple's free app for trying early versions of apps).
  2. Open the invitation link or the email invitation you were sent. It opens TestFlight.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. Bicoria appears like any other app.
  4. Try it out: add a bicycle with a photograph, record the odometer, log a service, fit a part, set a schedule and turn on reminders. If you run a workshop, try a Workshop with your team's names.
  5. To tell me what you think, open TestFlight, choose Bicoria and tap Send Beta Feedback, or email reallyusefulapps@outlook.com.

Test versions expire after 90 days, and TestFlight tells you when a newer one is ready. Your data stays on your device, and installing a newer test version keeps it.
